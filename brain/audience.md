# Audience — who is on the other side of the phone

*Read by `tools/brain.py` with business.md and strategy.md. Five people, not a
demographic. Every post is written to one of them by name; the gates do not
check this, the editor's judgement does.*

## 1. Ngozi, 29, Lagos (Yaba/Ikeja) — the renewing tenant
- Pays yearly, in cash or transfer, often without a stamped receipt. Her
  landlord "wants an increase" every January and talks about "packing out".
- Online: Instagram and Threads at lunch and 8–10 pm; WhatsApp status all day;
  reads Facebook for the comments.
- Pain words: *increase, packing out, agreement fee again, no receipt, agent*.
- Wins for her: the notice ladder, "renewal is not a new tenancy", the rent
  receipt lines, the caution-fee refund.
- Sends posts to: her flatmate, her sister, the office group.
- Buys the pass when: a renewal is due and the landlord has produced nothing in
  writing.

## 2. Tunde, 24, Abuja (Kubwa/Lugbe) — first flat, NYSC/new graduate
- Renting for the first time in August–October; being asked for "two years",
  "agreement + agency + caution" by an agent who may not be the landlord's agent.
- Online: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts in the evening; Telegram for
  jobs and alerts.
- Pain words: *agent, inspection fee, two years upfront, caution fee, scam*.
- Wins: "pay the wrong agent, lose the flat", what a tenancy agreement must
  contain, the receipt before the key.
- Sends posts to: the squad group, the person he is moving in with.
- Buys when: he needs a clean agreement the landlord will sign today.

## 3. Mr Okafor, 52, Enugu/Anambra/Port Harcourt — the small landlord
- Two to six units, no lawyer on retainer, burned once by a tenant who stayed
  rent-free for a year. Wants to "do it properly" and be respected.
- Online: Facebook (reads and comments at length), WhatsApp groups, YouTube
  long-form at night.
- Pain words: *owing tenant, court, quit notice, damage, proper document*.
- Wins: the correct notice for the correct tenancy, what a demand letter does,
  why a dated receipt protects him too, the state-specific rule.
- Shares posts to: landlord association group; comments with his own story.
- Buys when: he has to issue notices to more than one tenant or renew several
  agreements at once.

## 4. Amaka, 34, Lagos/Ibadan — the small-business owner
- Runs a shop, a salon, a logistics outfit or a 4-person studio. Pays staff
  informally, invoices by WhatsApp, has been stiffed by a client.
- Online: Instagram and WhatsApp Business in the day; Threads for vendor
  banter; Facebook for groups.
- Pain words: *client owing, no invoice, staff left with money, payslip for
  visa/loan, warning letter*.
- Wins: invoice, payslip, employment contract, warning letter, letter of
  demand — one job each.
- Sends posts to: other vendors; saves checklists.
- Buys when: a bank, embassy or client asks for a document she does not have.

## 5. Barr. Dayo / the estate agent, 38 — the professional reseller
- Prepares many documents a month for others; price-sensitive, time-poor,
  wants clean output with his firm's name on it (business licence).
- Online: LinkedIn, Facebook, Threads; evening YouTube.
- Pain words: *template, firm branding, bulk, turnaround*.
- Wins: speed, consistency, the state coverage, "two minutes".
- Buys: the business licence, not the ₦2,000 pass; never discount to reach him.

## Rules that follow from the people
- Write the post to one of the five. Name their situation in the first line;
  the law in the second; the paper in the last.
- The send line names the receiver: "send this to the friend renewing in
  January", not "share if you agree".
- The question is answerable in five words: "which state are you renting in?",
  "did your last receipt carry a date?".
- Pidgin for Tunde and market scenarios; Mr Okafor gets plain, respectful
  English with the section number; Amaka gets the document name in the
  headline.
- Evening posts (Instagram, Facebook Story) are for Ngozi and Tunde; the
  morning Facebook post is for Mr Okafor; lunch Threads is for Ngozi and Amaka;
  Telegram is for everyone who forwards.
