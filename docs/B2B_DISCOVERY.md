## 2. The wedge: the document layer, not property management

Full property-management suites already exist in Nigeria (OurProperty NG, RentalDesk,
SOUTECH Apps — rent collection, leases, maintenance, estates). DocOga does **not**
compete with them. The wedge is the **documents** those workflows keep producing:
tenancy agreements, rent receipts, quit notices, demand letters, caution-fee letters,
employment contracts, payslips, POA.

| LegalDoc, tenancy agreement / demand letter | **from ₦5,000** per document (80+ templates) | legaldoc.ng |

| Lawyer route, flat market fee | **₦15,000–₦30,000** "fair" flat fee; ₦20,000–₦80,000 observed range | nobroker.ng (C), cabans.ng (C) |

**DocOga's position:** Oga Pass = **₦2,000 / 30 days, all tools**. That is
1–2% of one flat-fee lawyer agreement, and roughly **40–75% less than a single
LawBridge/LegalDoc/LawDepot document** while covering a month of unlimited use.
An agency producing 10 agreements a month spends ₦20k–₦50k at per-document
competitors or ₦150k–₦300k via the flat-fee lawyer route — against one ₦2,000 pass.

**Honest caveat (Rule Zero):** per-document competitors are the *ceiling* of the
comparison, not the typical buyer. The NoBroker analysis (C) says most agreements
are template work sold at 5–10% of rent — i.e. the real competitor is "a Word file
and a ₦0 marginal cost", and the buyer who pays 10%-of-rent lawyer fees is paying
for review, not paper. The B2B sale is therefore **volume + consistency + state
correctness**, not "documents are impossible to get".

## 3. Target segments (tiered)

### 3.1 Estate agencies & property managers — PRIMARY

- **Regulated, findable, Lagos-first.** Lagos estate agents are regulated by
  **LASRERA** (Lagos State Real Estate Regulatory Authority); professional valuers
  fall under **NIESV/ESVARBON** (ESVARBON established by Decree No. 24 of 1975, now
  Cap E13 LFN 2007). [B]
- **Scale evidence.** NIESV's own publication puts registered estate surveyors and
  valuers at ~4,450, with "all manners of people encroaching" on the profession —
  i.e. practicing agents far outnumber registered professionals. A 2018 academic
  study counted **5,111 NIESV associate members after 40+ years** (ratio 1:38,309).
  [B/C, dated] **The exact number of estate agencies in Nigeria remains
  UNVERIFIED (E)** — see §8.
- **Pain evidence.** Academic assessment of Nigerian property management (2013,
  ResearchGate): disputes are mostly **rent default, rent increment, maintenance,
  conversion, illegal transfer, disturbance**; recommended practice is aggressive
  **demand notices / letters of reminder** and proper filing systems. [B, dated]
  Industry sources describe the same operational pain at 50–100+ units: late rent,
  manual receipt books, missed follow-ups, disputes. [C]
- **Fee-salience.** Lagos caps agency fee at **10% of annual rent** and the
  legal/agreement fee at 10% (Lagos State Tenancy Law 2011, as reported by multiple
  guides). [B/C] Document costs are a visible, capped line item in every Lagos
  tenancy — which makes a fixed-price document pass an easy line to defend.
- **Legislative tailwind (labelled):** the Lagos State Tenancy and Recovery of
  Premises Bill 2025 (draft) passed second reading in July 2025 — it would regulate
  landlord-tenant relations, rent structures and eviction. **It is a bill, not law.**
  [B]

### 3.2 Landlord/estate bodies & associations — PRIMARY (channel, not yet buyers)

- **Landlords & Tenants Rights Initiative (LTR)** — CAC-registered NGO since 2009,
  Abuja; takes complaints from both parties, runs ADR with the Standing Conference
  of Mediation Advocates. landlordsandtenants.org. [B]
- **Tenants & Residents Associations (TRAs)** operate across Lagos, Abuja, Port
  Harcourt; they negotiate with landlords/property managers as collectives.
  [C]
- NIESV state branches and ESVARBON zonal offices are member bodies of the exact
  primary segment. [B]
- These are **channels to reach landlords/agencies**, and potential bulk buyers
  themselves (an association buying passes for its members).

### 3.3 HR-adjacent SMEs — SECONDARY

- Every SME hiring needs employment contracts, offer letters, payslips — DocOga
  ships employment agreement + payslip tools.
- The HR suite market is crowded (HRPayHub ₦499/user/month, PaidHR, BridgeHR, Zoho
  People, Sage HR — punchng.com list, C), but the **document-bundle** position is
  proven: FormWork sells a 35+ document HR bundle at ₦30,000 starter / ₦100,000
  growth. [A-vendor]
- Weaker wedge than property (full HRIS suites bundle documents); keep as segment 2.

### 3.4 Co-working / serviced-office operators — TERTIARY

- Membership agreements, licences, house rules — plausible, no evidence gathered
  this pass. [E] Listed for completeness; do not prioritise over 3.1–3.3.

## 6. Discovery question set (ask, don't pitch)

1. How many tenancy agreements / receipts / quit notices / demand letters do you
   produce in a typical month?
2. Who prepares them today — lawyer, agent with a template, typist — and what does
   it cost you **per document**?
3. Which document causes the most trouble: agreement, receipt, quit notice,
   demand letter, renewal?
4. Do you work across states (Lagos only, or Lagos + Abuja + others)? Do you need
   state-specific agreements?
5. How many people on your team would use it?
6. Would you pay by bank transfer for a monthly team pass? (Businesses pay by
   transfer — no card needed.)
7. **WTP probe (ask last, offer no anchor first):** "If a 30-day pass covered every
   document your team produces, what would feel like a fair monthly price for your
   volume?" — record the number verbatim.
