## 1. The decision (and the research behind it)

**What we're building:** DocOga — a Nigeria-focused document engine for landlords,
tenants and small businesses. Three tools at launch: **Tenancy Agreement**, **Rent
Receipt**, **Invoice**. Free watermarked versions win search traffic; the **₦2,000
Oga Pass** (30 days, sold via Selar) delivers clean, banner-free documents.

**Why this niche won the research:**

| Finding | Evidence | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Plain invoice generators are saturated & free | EasyBill, NaijaSabi, InvoiceGenerator.ng all offer free, no-watermark tools for Nigerians | Invoice alone = no moat, no money. We keep it only as a free traffic magnet |
| Legal/business documents are proven moneymakers in Nigeria | Wonder.legal charges Nigerians per customized tenancy agreement & rent receipt; lawyers charge ₦20,000–₦50,000 per draft | Willingness-to-pay is proven. We undercut by 10–25× |
| The need is recurring | Nigerian rent is paid annually in advance — agreements, receipts and renewals recur every year | Natural repeat purchases → subscription logic |
| Search demand exists with weak local competition | "tenancy agreement Nigeria", "rent receipt format" etc. are actively searched; most results are jumbled PDF blogs | A clean, fast, mobile-first tool can rank and convert |
| Name collisions avoided | "NaijaDocs" (US medical app), "OgaDoc" (Nigerian telehealth) already exist; **DocOga is clear** | Brand is defensible & searchable |

**Domain status (checked 18 Sep 2026):** docoga.com ✅ available · docoga.com.ng ✅ · docoga.xyz ✅

## 2. Business model

- **Free tier:** all 3 tools, unlimited use, small DocOga banner on documents. Zero
  signup. Data stays on the user's device (privacy = trust + zero server cost).
- **Oga Pass — ₦2,000 / 30 days:** banner-free unlimited documents, priority
  WhatsApp support, early access to new document types. Sold via Selar
  (they charge ≈ 4% + ₦50; you net ≈ ₦1,870 per pass).
- **Unit economics:** ~100% gross margin. The product costs ₦0/month to run
  (single-file static app on free hosting). Every pass sold is almost pure profit.
- **Break-even:** ONE Oga Pass covers the domain name.

## 3. The moat ladder (how you avoid being pushed out)

Copycats can clone a form in a weekend. They cannot clone:

1. **Rung 1 — Speed & niche depth (now):** Be the best tenancy-agreement tool in
   Nigeria. State-specific content (36 states + FCT), pidgin-friendly voice,
   mobile-first UX tuned for Nigerian phones.
2. **Rung 2 — SEO authority (months 1–9):** Every article, FAQ and tool page
   compounds into Google rankings. A copycat starting month 9 starts from zero.
3. **Rung 3 — Accounts + data lock-in (v2):** User accounts, saved properties,
   tenants, agreements + **tenancy-expiry reminder alerts** (WhatsApp/email).
   Renewals happen every year — reminders make DocOga the system of record.
   Switching cost becomes real.
4. **Rung 4 — Two-sided network (v3):** Verified landlord/agent directory +
   tenant screening. Each side makes the other more valuable — the classic
   "can't be pushed out" position.
5. **Rung 5 — Payments (v4):** Rent collection via licensed rails (Paystack
   etc.). Highest value, most regulated — only after scale.

## 9. Market reality — the data (verified 18 Sep 2026)

- **Nigerians already PAY for tenancy agreements.** Lagos regulation caps tenancy
  "agreement/legal fees" at 10% of annual rent — agents and landlords routinely
  charge it (₦50k–₦150k on a ₦1.5M rent). DocOga sells the same document at ₦2,000.
- **Scale:** official 2025 housing deficit = 14.9M units + 15.2M substandard homes
  (Federal Ministry of Housing). Lagos alone: 3.4M-unit deficit; 15M+ renters
  (~60–70% of residents); rents rising ~18%/year.
- **Recurring engine:** rent is paid 1+ year in advance — every cycle needs
  agreements, receipts, renewals. 70% of Lagos tenants face deposit/landlord
  disputes (LASRERA) — disputes drive document demand.
- **Payment rails proven:** Nigeria e-payments hit ₦1.08 quadrillion in 2024
  (+80% YoY); ₦285 trillion in Q1 2025 alone; 64.9M BVN enrolments.
- **Funnel:** Lagos ≈ 3M renting households, each needing 1+ agreement/year.
  Capturing 0.1% = 3,000 passes = ₦6M/yr (Lagos alone). 1% = ₦60M/yr.
- **Honest counterforce:** the real competitor is informality — no document at
  all, or a friend's copied PDF. The dispute statistics are why that behavior
  changes, and why tenant-rights content converts.
- **Power segment:** estate agents — multiple deals monthly, already charging
  clients agreement fees. Highest-frequency users of the tools.

The market exists and pays today. The only open question is reach — §4.
