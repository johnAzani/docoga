# DocOga discovery workbook — ready for owner review

10 October 2026. Companion: `B2B_OUTREACH_GROUNDWORK.md` contains source evidence, capability limits and legal/privacy caveats. **Templates, not messages sent. Business unpriced; no USD work.**

## 1. First screen for the owner

Do you already know an estate agent, property manager, landlord with several rentals, or someone who can introduce you to the person preparing their paperwork?

Only relationship and role are needed now. Do not paste phone numbers, customer lists, agreements or payment details. If yes, draft one request suited to the existing relationship; if no, use your own opt-in invitation or a verified channel's permitted recruitment route.

## 2. Message library — choose by permission context

### A. Warm introducer request

Use only in an existing relationship where this request is appropriate. Do not cold-message a scraped contact with this template.

> I'm researching how rental businesses prepare everyday paperwork for DocOga. Do you know someone who actually handles their receipts or tenancy documents? If so, please ask whether they'd be comfortable with a 15–20 minute conversation with me. No purchase is required. Please don't send me their number unless they've agreed to the introduction.

### B. Forwardable invitation / own business-profile post

Post only on your own appropriate channel or with administrator permission. Do not tag prospects or DM everyone who views it.

> Do you prepare rent receipts or tenancy paperwork for a property business? I'm John, building DocOga in Abuja. I'm inviting a few practitioners to describe how they handle this work today—what works well and what doesn't. It's a 15–20 minute research conversation, not a request to buy. No tenant names, documents or payment details needed. If you're interested, message me “document research”. I'll explain the conversation and ask permission before any follow-up.

Do not promise free legal review, free lifetime passes, payment for interviews, or exclusive early access that doesn't exist.

### C. Response to someone who opted in

> Thanks for volunteering. I'd like to understand the last rental document you prepared, your current process and any difficulties. I can take brief notes without client identities; I won't record the call or add you to marketing. You can skip questions or stop. Would you prefer a short call, voice notes or text, and is brief note-taking okay?

For a call, get explicit permission and a time. A call preference is not permission for repeated unsolicited calls. If text is preferred, send one question at a time.

### D. Association/channel request — through a legitimate, permitted route

> I'm John from DocOga, a self-help document tool—not a law firm. We're researching how property administrators prepare routine rental paperwork. Would your association consider allowing a short invitation for volunteers through an appropriate member channel? We are not requesting a member list or your endorsement. Participation would be optional, no purchase required, and no client documents collected. I can send the invitation for your review first.

Don't send this through a property valuation, job application, emergency complaint or tenant-support form. Don't imply membership or partnership. Confirm any cost; no paid access is authorised.

### E. Interview opening

> Thank you. This is research for DocOga, which may inform a future commercial offering, but I won't ask you to buy today. Please keep client names, contact details and private documents out of the conversation. Is it okay to take short notes? What is your role in preparing or approving rental paperwork?

If notes declined, respect it. If participant starts showing client data, ask to switch to a made-up example; do not capture a screenshot.

### F. Optional task-test invitation after evidence

> You mentioned that [their specific task] is difficult because [their explanation]. Would you be willing to try a made-up example in your usual tool and then in DocOga? It would help us see whether there is any real improvement. No real tenant data or purchase is needed. It's fine if your current method is better.

Fill brackets only from what was actually said. Agree scope, date, duration and notes permission. No implied free premium entitlement.

### G. One previously agreed follow-up

> Hi, following up once on the document-research conversation you agreed to. Is it still useful to arrange it, or should I close this for now? Either is fine.

Only if follow-up was agreed; normally 3–5 working days later, adjusted to their request. No reply: stop. Refusal: thank them briefly if appropriate, mark do-not-contact, no other channel chase.

### H. If they ask the price

> The existing Oga Pass is ₦2,000 for 30 days. We're not quoting a separate Business package in this research. First I want to understand your workflow and whether anything beyond the current product is actually needed. I won't promise team controls, logos or other features we haven't demonstrated.

Record prior awareness of retail price. Do not hide the public price to protect a hypothesis. Explain expiry accurately rather than relying on older terms wording.

### I. If they ask whether it replaces a lawyer

> No. DocOga provides self-help drafts, not legal advice or a lawyer–client relationship. Your review process still matters. For legal notices, unusual terms or a dispute, please involve a qualified Nigerian lawyer.

### J. Close and permission

> My understanding is [task], [frequency] and [difficulty—or no difficulty]. What did I misunderstand? Is there one next step you would find useful? May I contact you only about that step?

No next step is a valid answer. Separate permission is needed to quote them publicly or use their company logo.

## 4. Synthetic task card

**Task:** draft a fictional receipt recording a payment already confirmed in the fictional scenario.

- Landlord/issuer: Example Property Office (fictional)
- Tenant: Sample Tenant (fictional)
- Property: Unit A, Example Building, Abuja (fictional, not a real address)
- Rent: ₦100,000 (test amount, not price research)
- Payment date: 10 October 2026
- Rent period: 1 October–31 October 2026
- Payment method: bank transfer (fictional; no banking details)
- Purpose: test form accuracy and output only; never used as evidence of payment.

**Instructions:** reproduce these facts using their normal method, then a comparable fictional example using DocOga. The output is for demonstration only and must be discarded after review. Do not share it with a real tenant or pretend a transfer occurred.

**Acceptance checklist:** correct names, property, amount, date, period, method, no invented payment confirmation, readable output, no truncation, accessible export/print and participant's reviewer agrees it fits the intended administrative task. Compare minutes including corrections and help. Do not count 'looked nice' as accepted production output.

For a second task, change names/amount/period and use no help. Compare task order across participants. No pass issuance or payment is part of this exercise.

## 5. Procurement questionnaire — only for an interested approver

Ask only the questions relevant to the agreed scope, after basic discovery:

- Which legal entity buys? Who has signing and budget authority?
- What is the last similar purchase process? Invoice/PO/vendor registration requirements?
- Preferred billing cadence and payment method, without offering a price yet?
- Is document review by counsel required? Who approves templates?
- Is single-user browser storage acceptable? If not, what data/control obligation drives that?
- Must access be revoked by employee? Is central history or auditability mandatory?
- What security/privacy information does procurement need?
- What support response is essential rather than merely preferred?
- How will both sides decide whether the bounded pilot worked?

Do not collect tax IDs, account numbers or signed procurement documents in discovery chat. DocOga's legal entity, invoice/tax readiness, commercial terms and fulfillment capacity must be verified before contracting. Do not invent certifications or service-level guarantees.

## 7. Free tools and data boundaries

- Existing WhatsApp Business app for permissioned one-to-one conversations; no API, paid automation, broadcast service or browser bot.
- Owner's private notes/spreadsheet for consent/contact mapping. Access-limited, not shared via public link.
- Repository templates contain headers only. No personal data, contact numbers, interview quotes, agreements or payment identifiers.
- Agent can help organise redacted findings and update the evidence matrix. Owner performs approved conversations and screenshot-based account steps when needed.
- If a screenshot is used, crop names, numbers, client data and document identifiers. Do not assume a browser history or chat list is automatically safe.
