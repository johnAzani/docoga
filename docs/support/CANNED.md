## Escalation rule (owner)

Any email that says: money moved but no record appeared, a team is stuck
unpaid with a valid proof of transfer, or a customer is unhappy twice — stop
the canned reply, look at the worker KV (invoice + team records) first, fix
the product if it's a product bug, and reply with the concrete result. The
canned text is for the normal 80%; the other 20% is where the revenue comes
from or is lost.
