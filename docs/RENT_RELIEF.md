# Rent relief (NTA 2025) — the page, the receipt fields, the season

**Owner's "Go", Tue 27 Oct 2026, ~3:00pm, on Strategy Office analysis #1 (DO NOW #1).** ₦0.

## Why

From the 2026 year of assessment every Nigerian tenant may deduct **20% of annual rent paid,
maximum ₦500,000**, from taxable income — Nigeria Tax Act 2025, **s.30(2)(a)(vi)** — *"provided
that the individual accurately declares the actual amount of rent paid and other relevant
information as may be prescribed by the relevant tax authority"*. It is not automatic; with no
declaration the relief is ₦0. Employees hand evidence to payroll (the NRS: "if you pay your rent
in January and provide your receipt, the employer can start deducting … even before the year
ends"); the self-employed submit receipts/agreement to the State IRS. Employer annual returns
are due 31 Jan 2027, individual returns 31 Mar 2027. Evidence asked for in practice: a rent
receipt in the tenant's name (date, amount, property, period), a signed tenancy agreement,
proof of payment, and the landlord's name and TIN/NIN. UNILAG required all staff to attach
"evidence of rent payment … [that] must indicate the name and address of applicant" by
13 Feb 2026.

DocOga already makes the receipt and the agreement for free. Until today nothing on the site
said so, and the receipt had no landlord-TIN or payer-address line.
