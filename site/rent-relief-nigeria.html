Rent relief in Nigeria — the 20% deduction every tenant can claim from 2026, and the papers you need

The Nigeria Tax Act 2025 scrapped the old consolidated relief and gave tenants something better: a deduction of 20% of the rent you actually pay, up to ₦500,000 a year. It is not automatic. Nobody gets it without the paper — a rent receipt in your name, a tenancy agreement, and your landlord's details. Here is the law, the arithmetic, the deadlines, and a free letter that puts it all in front of your payroll desk.

What the law says

Section 30(2)(a)(vi) of the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 allows, in computing chargeable income, a rent relief of 20% of annual rent paid, subject to a maximum of ₦500,000, whichever is lower — "provided that the individual accurately declares the actual amount of rent paid and other relevant information as may be prescribed by the relevant tax authority". It applies from the 2026 year of assessment (1 January 2026) to employees under PAYE and to the self-employed assessed directly.

  • Only tenants. Homeowners and people in employer-provided accommodation do not qualify.
  • Only rent actually paid. Prepaid rent counts only to the extent it has been paid; rent that straddles two years is counted for the months in the year being assessed.
  • Joint tenants each claim on their own share, each up to the ₦500,000 cap.
  • The cap bites at ₦2,500,000 of rent — above that, more rent earns no more relief.

What the tax authorities' own guidelines say

The Joint Revenue Board of Nigeria (the body of all State tax authorities and the NRS) issued Personal Income Tax Guidelines, 2026 (dated 24 February 2026). Part III, paragraph 9 is the rent relief, and it settles the questions people argue about:

  • 9.1–9.2 — 20% of annual rent paid, maximum ₦500,000; the actual rent paid "and other prescribed information must be accurately declared".
  • 9.3 Shared tenancy — each tenant claims up to ₦500,000 on their own portion of the rent. Two flatmates on ₦3,000,000 each claim on ₦1,500,000 → ₦300,000 each.
  • 9.4–9.5 Actual-year basis — rent paid in 2026 relieves 2026; rent spanning years is apportioned. The Board's own example: ₦2,000,000 paid for 24 months counts ₦1,000,000 for 2026, relief ₦200,000.
  • 9.6 Minimum information — (i) the legal tenant's name; (ii) actual rent paid and the period covered; (iii) the landlord's full name and one of mobile number, Tax ID or NIN; (iv) the property address.

That fourth paragraph is the test your receipt has to pass. A receipt that names the tenant, states the rent and the period, carries the landlord's name with a phone number, TIN or NIN, and gives the property address meets it. Payroll systems (SeamlessHR's own FAQ says so) hold the number you declare; the paper is still yours to bring, and HR may require it uploaded before the relief is applied.

  • A rent receipt in your name showing the amount, the period covered, the property address and the landlord's full name with a phone number, TIN or NIN — the four particulars the Joint Revenue Board's guidelines list (para 9.6, below). The DocOga receipt tells you when all four are on it.
  • A signed tenancy agreement covering the year, naming you, the landlord, the premises and the rent.

How and when to claim

  • Employees (PAYE): hand the letter and attachments to HR / payroll. The NRS has said that if you pay your rent in January and provide the receipt, the employer can apply the relief in your monthly tax immediately. Employers file their annual PAYE return by 31 January 2027.
  • Self-employed, traders and artisans: submit the receipts / agreement with your annual return to your State Internal Revenue Service by 31 March 2027 for the 2026 year.
  • Missed it in payroll? You can still claim the balance in your own annual return by 31 March of the following year.
  • Keep everything six years. Tax records must be retained; a receipt you can verify online (every DocOga receipt carries a code) is the easiest to defend.

Questions people ask

Is rent relief automatic in my salary?

No. Under s.30 of the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 the relief is granted only when you accurately declare the rent actually paid, with evidence. If you declare nothing, the relief is ₦0 — there is no fallback figure.

What documents do I need for rent relief?

A rent receipt in your name carrying the four particulars in para 9.6 of the Joint Revenue Board's PIT Guidelines 2026 — your name, the rent and period covered, the landlord's full name with a phone number, TIN or NIN, and the property address — plus a signed tenancy agreement covering the year, proof of payment such as the bank transfer, and a signed declaration to your employer or tax office.

My landlord will not give me a receipt. Can I still claim?

Insist, in writing — in Lagos a landlord who refuses a rent receipt commits an offence (Tenancy Law 2011, s.5(3), ₦100,000 fine). Practitioners report that a signed confirmation letter from the landlord plus your bank transfer evidence can stand in, but with no evidence at all there is no relief for that period.

I paid two years' rent in 2025. What can I claim in 2026?

Relief is on an actual-year basis: only the part of the rent attributable to the 2026 year of assessment counts, and only if it was actually paid. Use the months selector in the calculator above.
