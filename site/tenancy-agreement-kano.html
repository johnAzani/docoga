What probably governs recovery of possession instead

Kano was created out of the former Northern Region. The peer-reviewed account of how Nigerian tenancy law travelled is that the regions each reproduced the original Recovery of Premises Ordinance, and that on the creation of twelve states in 1968 each new state inherited all the existing laws of the region it was carved from — later states inheriting in turn, mutatis mutandis, under the States Creation and Transitional Provisions Act.

This is inference, not confirmation. It rests on the constitutional framework for the succession of regional laws, not on a Kano statute or a Kano judgment we have read. We publish it as the most likely starting point and say plainly that it is unconfirmed. Do not rely on it without local advice.

The Northern Region’s recovery law is cited as the Recovery of Premises Law, Laws of Northern Nigeria 1963 — given as Cap. 113, Vol. 3 in one source and as Cap. 115 in another. Those citations conflict and we have not resolved them.

Other protections worth knowing

  • A landlord may not resort to self-help; unlawful eviction is actionable in damages.
  • The High Court has jurisdiction to entertain recovery proceedings in every state, whatever the lower-court position.
  • Improvements made with the landlord’s written consent may found a counterclaim for their unexhausted value.

What your Kano State agreement should lock down

  • The notice period you actually want. Courts uphold what the agreement says — the statutory defaults apply only “where there is no stipulation”. If you want three months, write three months.
  • The rent and the advance — exact amount in figures and words, and how much is payable in advance.
  • The caution fee — amount, purpose, and the refund terms. This is the most disputed money in Nigerian tenancies.
  • The parties and the property — full names, addresses, and a clear description of the premises.
  • Covenants — repairs, utilities, subletting, use of the premises, and renewal terms.

Questions people ask

Is a written tenancy agreement compulsory in Kano State?

A tenancy can exist without one — but without it you have no agreed rent record, no caution-fee terms, and the statutory notice defaults govern. A two-minute written agreement is cheaper than any dispute.

Does the agreement override the notice periods in the law?

Generally yes — the statutory periods apply where the agreement is silent. Write the notice you want into the agreement.
