Frequently asked questions

Is a DocOga tenancy agreement valid in Nigeria?

A tenancy agreement becomes legally binding when it is properly filled, signed by both landlord and tenant, and witnessed. DocOga drafts follow standard Nigerian practice. DocOga is not a law firm and does not give legal advice — for unusual or high-value deals, have a lawyer review before signing.

How much does a lawyer charge for a tenancy agreement?

Depending on the state and property, typically ₦20,000–₦50,000 or more. DocOga gives you the standard document free (with a small banner), or clean unlimited copies with the Oga Pass.

What is a caution fee / security deposit?

A caution fee is a refundable deposit (often 5–10% of rent) that protects the landlord against damage beyond normal wear and tear. It must be refunded at the end of the tenancy if the property is returned in good condition. DocOga documents it inside the agreement.

How many months' notice to quit?

It depends on the tenancy type and the state. In Lagos, the Tenancy Law 2011 provides 3 months' notice for yearly tenants and 1 month for monthly tenants. DocOga's notice tool computes the vacation date for you.
