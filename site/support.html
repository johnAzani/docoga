Support

DocOga · Business teams · Abuja, Nigeria

The short version: everything a team needs is self-serve in your team dashboard — seats, branding, invoices, payment. For anything else, email business@docoga.com.ng — an automated inbox that answers with exactly what you need: payment, the team dashboard, and the answers below. No WhatsApp needed for Business teams.

Questions teams ask most

What does the Business licence include?

An annual, per-team licence: your firm’s name and logo appear on every document instead of DocOga’s, named team seats you invite and remove by email, a documents-per-month allowance, and one annual invoice with VAT. Activation is fully automatic — the moment payment lands, the licence switches on.

How do I pay?

From your team dashboard: by card / USSD through our licensed payment provider (Flutterwave), or by bank transfer to a one-time virtual account we create for your exact amount. Your team activates by itself the moment the exact amount lands — nothing to chase, nothing to confirm.

I transferred a wrong amount — what happens?

Your money is safe. The licence does not activate, you will get an email within a few minutes with a fresh account number for the correct amount, and the old one stops working. If you need help sorting it, reply to that email.

How do I invite or remove team members?

Team dashboard → Team seats (owner only). Invite with their email — they receive a sign-in link by email (magic link, no password). Removing someone is one tap; their previously created documents are unaffected, they simply lose the firm banner. Adding seats beyond your licence’s count mid-year goes through business@docoga.com.ng — a human confirms it by email.

How do I get my invoice as a PDF?

Team dashboard → Invoices & payments → Open / Save PDF. Each invoice opens as a printable page with your firm’s branding — press the print button (or your browser’s Print → Save as PDF). Invoices stay available in the dashboard for 12 months; for older ones, email business@docoga.com.ng.

How do I change my firm’s name or logo?

Team dashboard → Firm branding (owner only). Name, tagline, address and footer line are text fields; the logo accepts PNG, JPG or SVG up to 25KB. Press Save branding and it applies to every document the team produces from that moment.

My licence is expiring — how do renewals work?

We email you as your licence approaches (about 90, 30 and 7 days before). Renewals are self-serve: Team dashboard → Renew — bank transfer or card, at the same price you paid, and your team renews automatically when payment lands. The renewal window opens 90 days before expiry, and early renewal stacks on your remaining days — none are lost. Nothing is ever switched off in the middle of a year without notice.

If you miss the expiry: there is a 14-day grace period (branding pauses, nothing is deleted), then the team moves to the free tier and its data (branding, seats, invoices) is kept 90 days. Renewing at any of those points restores everything at your purchase price.

Refunds?

Send a refund request to business@docoga.com.ng — every request is kept on record. Because the licence activates immediately and documents can be produced right away, we do not promise a fixed refund window.

Where is my data stored?

Documents are created entirely on each member’s own device and are never uploaded or stored by us — same as the free tools. The team account itself stores the firm name, branding, seat emails and your invoice/payment status. Full detail: Privacy Policy (section 5).

How do you respond?

DocOga Business is fully self-serve: email business@docoga.com.ng and the automated reply points you to payment, the team dashboard and this FAQ — instantly, no queue. Oga Pass individuals: use the WhatsApp button on the pricing page.
