Abuja tenant: Your renewal agreement ready?

This week, talk of rent relief reminds us: paper beats memory.

In Abuja, your landlord can ask for any amount of advance rent, but whatever you agree,

put it in writing before you pay.

From 2026, you can claim 20% rent relief on your taxes (up to ₦500k) with a proper receipt and agreement

. Don't leave money on the table. Your tenancy agreement is the first step.
