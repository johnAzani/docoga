Rivers tenants: Your quit notice date is specific

The law behind it

  • In Rivers State, your landlord's notice to quit must end on the eve of the anniversary of your tenancy term.
    s.7, Recovery of Premises Law — This is the Rivers rule that most affects real cases, and it is the opposite of Lagos. s.7: a notice to quit must terminate on the eve of the anniversary of the current term. The notice must end on the day before the next term would renew, and it must cover the full statutory length between the date of service and that eve.
  • A notice that gets this date wrong is defective.
    s.7, Recovery of Premises Law — Lagos s.13(4) is the exception: three- and six-month notices there need not end on the anniversary, and may end on or after the term expires. In Rivers — and, on the practitioner account, in most Nigerian states except Lagos — the eve-of-anniversary rule applies. Get the date wrong and the notice is defective.
  • If your tenancy started January 1st, a yearly notice must expire December 31st.
    s.7, Recovery of Premises Law — A yearly tenancy that began on 1 January 2025 runs to 31 December 2025. A valid six-month quit notice must expire on that 31 December — the eve of the anniversary — not a week either side of it.
  • A defective notice protects you from having to move out based on incorrect paperwork.
    Recovery of Premises Law — A defect in the issue, content or service of a notice vitiates the whole proceeding.
