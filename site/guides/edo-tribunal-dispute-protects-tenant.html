A dispute at the tribunal pauses your quit notice

In Edo State, once a tenant applies to the Rent Tribunal,

any quit notice served afterwards has no effect under section 16.

A landlord cannot use a retaliatory notice to force you out while the tribunal is hearing your case.

Know your rights before an agent uses threats to push you out.

The law behind it

  • In Edo State, once a tenant applies to the Rent Tribunal, any quit notice served afterwards has no effect under section 16.
    s.16, Rent Control and Recovery of Residential Premises Edict 1977 — Edo’s strongest tenant protection, and one of the strongest in the country. s.16(1): once a tenant has applied to the tribunal, any quit notice served afterwards is of no effect, and no similar notice may be given before the tribunal’s decision.
