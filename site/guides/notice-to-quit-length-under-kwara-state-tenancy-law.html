Notice To Quit Length Under Kwara State Tenancy Law

Many tenants in Kwara wake up to sudden eviction notices demanding they leave within a few days. Landlords often assume any short letter is enough to clear a house, but the law sets a clear ladder for how much time you must receive before leaving.

1. Tenancy at will or weekly tenancy requires one week notice to quit (s.17(1)(a)).

2. Monthly tenancy requires one month notice to quit (s.17(1)(b)).

3. Quarterly tenancy requires a quarter notice to quit (s.17(1)(c)).

4. Yearly tenancy requires half a year notice to quit (s.17(1)(d)).

Check your original tenancy agreement or receipts to confirm how your rent is paid, count the calendar months properly, and do not pack out in a panic if the days given fall short of what the law demands.

The law behind it
