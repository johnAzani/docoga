Did your Lagos landlord issue a separate receipt for caution fee?

In Lagos, where a landlord requires a security deposit, the landlord or agent must issue a separate receipt.

The tenant is entitled to a written account at least every six months of how the deposit money was disbursed.

The law behind it

  • In Lagos, where a landlord requires a security deposit, the landlord or agent must issue a separate receipt.
    s.5, Tenancy Law of Lagos State 2011 — s.10 — deposits and service charges. Where the landlord requires a security deposit, payment for services and facilities, or service charges in a block with common parts, the landlord or agent must issue a separate receipt, and the tenant is entitled to a written account at least every six months of how the money was disbursed.
