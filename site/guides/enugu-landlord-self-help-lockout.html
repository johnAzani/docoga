What happens when an Enugu landlord changes your lock

A tenant in Enugu came home to find his lock changed and electricity cut off because rent expired.

Many landlords think taking off doors or locking tenants out is a quick way to reclaim property.

In Enugu, a landlord cannot resort to self-help like lock-outs, removing roofs, or cutting power.

Unlawful eviction makes the landlord liable for damages in court, regardless of tenant status.

The law behind it

  • A landlord in Enugu cannot resort to self-help like lock-outs, removing roofs, or cutting power.
    Landlord and Tenant Law — A landlord may not resort to self-help — no lock-outs, no removal of goods or roofs, no disconnection. Unlawful eviction sounds in damages (Ihenacho v. Uzochukwu (1997) 1 SCNJ 117; Chicason Group v. Otero [2021] 6 NWLR (Pt. 1772) 222).
  • Unlawful eviction makes the landlord liable for damages in court, regardless of tenant status.
    Landlord and Tenant Law — No eviction without a court order, whatever the tenant’s status. The Court of Appeal in Chicason Group of Co. Ltd v. Otero [2021] 6 NWLR (Pt. 1772) 222: a tenant, lawful or illegal, cannot be ejected contrary to legal process, and the landlord or agent who does so is liable in damages — even a trespasser must be dealt with according to law.
