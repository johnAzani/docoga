How many months' rent can your landlord demand in Cross River?

Many tenants pay a full year upfront, but the law in Cross River State has a limit.

For individuals, landlords can only ask for up to three months' rent in advance.

This is much stricter than what you might find in other states like Lagos.

Knowing your rights can help you plan your rent payments better.

The law behind it

  • In Cross River State, a landlord may demand and receive the prescribed rent in advance for a period not exceeding three months from tenants who are individuals.
    s.6(6), Rent Control and Recovery of Premises Law — Cross River is the strictest jurisdiction we have verified — stricter than Lagos. s.6(6): a landlord may demand and receive the prescribed rent in advance for a period not exceeding three months from tenants who are individuals, and twelve months from tenants other than individuals.
