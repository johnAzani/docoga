The law behind it

  • For Kwara State, court no fit just throw tenant outside unless suitable alternative accommodation dey for ground.
    s.26, Rent Control and Recovery of Residential Premises Law 1995 — Kwara is the only jurisdiction we verified where the Court must be satisfied about alternative accommodation. s.26(1): no order for possession or ejectment may be made unless the tribunal or court considers it reasonable and either it has power under the Second Schedule, or it is satisfied that suitable alternative accommodation is available for the tenant.
  • Accommodation must fit tenant work place, pocket, and family size.
    s.26, Rent Control and Recovery of Residential Premises Law 1995 — s.26(2): accommodation is “suitable” where it is reasonably suitable to the needs of the tenant and his family as regards proximity to place of work, to the means of the tenant, and as to extent and character.
