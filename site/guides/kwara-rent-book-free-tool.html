How to get your rent book in Kwara

The law behind it

  • Landlords in Kwara's high density areas must issue a rent book to tenants, in addition to receipts.
    s.32, Rent Control and Recovery of Residential Premises Law 1995 — Kwara is one of the few states with a rent book duty. s.32(1): as from commencement, all landlords of premises in the high density areas to which the Law applies shall issue prescribed rent books in addition to receipts. s.32(2): the rent book is kept in the tenant’s custody and the landlord makes the entries in it when rent is paid.
  • The rent book stays with the tenant, and the landlord makes entries when rent is paid.
    s.32, Rent Control and Recovery of Residential Premises Law 1995 — s.32(3): a landlord who fails to issue the prescribed rent book is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine of ₦2,000 or imprisonment for up to three months, or both.
