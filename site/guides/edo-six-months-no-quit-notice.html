Edo: 6 Months No Quit Notice If You Pay In Advance

Ngozi, if you just paid your rent in Edo State, know your right.

The law says your landlord cannot issue a notice to quit for the first six months of your tenancy,

if you paid in advance (s.18).

This gives you statutory breathing space, except by tribunal order or your consent.

Don't let anyone push you out before time.

The law behind it

  • In Edo State, if you pay rent in advance for a monthly or quarterly tenancy, your landlord cannot give you a notice to quit or recover possession during the first six months.
    s.18 proviso, Rent Control and Recovery of Residential Premises Edict 1977 — Where rent is payable in advance for a monthly or quarterly tenancy, the landlord may not give notice to quit or recover possession during the first six months of the tenancy — except on the order of the tribunal or with the tenant’s consent. A new Edo tenant who has paid in advance has a six-month statutory breathing space.
