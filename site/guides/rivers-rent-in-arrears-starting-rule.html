Did your Rivers tenant pay before or after?

Many landlords in Port Harcourt expect one or two years of rent upfront before handing over keys.

Under Rivers State law, rent is payable in arrears at the end of the period unless you agreed otherwise.

The law behind it

  • Under Rivers State law, rent is payable in arrears at the end of the period unless you agreed otherwise.
    Landlord and Tenant Law, Cap. 75, s.75(1), Recovery of Premises Law — Rivers reverses the assumption most Nigerian tenants make. s.75(1) of the Landlord and Tenant Law: rent is payable in arrears — at the end of the period — unless the parties agree otherwise.
