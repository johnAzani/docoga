Your Lagos rent receipt is worth up to ₦500,000 in tax relief.

Ngozi, from 2026, you can claim 20% of your rent paid as tax relief, up to ₦500,000.

But only with a valid rent receipt in your name, with all four details HR needs.

In Lagos, your landlord MUST give you a receipt (s.5, Tenancy Law 2011).

It must show date, names, address, amount, and period to claim your relief.

The law behind it

  • From 2026, you can deduct 20% of your annual rent paid, up to ₦500,000, from your taxable income, but only if you have a valid rent receipt in your name.
    s.30(2)(a)(vi), Nigeria Tax Act 2025 — s.30(2)(a)(vi) Nigeria Tax Act 2025 (in force 1 January 2026): in computing the taxable income of an individual, a rent relief of 20% of the annual rent paid, subject to a maximum of ₦500,000, is deducted — provided the individual accurately declares the actual amount of rent paid and other relevant information the relevant tax authority prescribes.
  • In Lagos, every landlord must issue a receipt on payment of rent, stating the date, names and addresses of landlord and tenant, description and location of premises, amount paid, and period covered.
    s.5, Tenancy Law of Lagos State 2011 — Lagos is one of the few states that makes a rent receipt a statutory duty and prescribes what must be on it. s.5(1): every landlord shall issue a receipt on payment of rent. s.5(2): the receipt must state —
  • The receipt must state the date on which the rent was received.
    s.5(2), Tenancy Law of Lagos State 2011 — s.5(2): the receipt must state the date on which the rent was received.
  • The receipt must state the names and addresses of the landlord and the tenant.
    s.5(2), Tenancy Law of Lagos State 2011 — s.5(2): the receipt must state the names and addresses of the landlord and the tenant.
  • The receipt must state the description and location of the premises.
    s.5(2), Tenancy Law of Lagos State 2011 — s.5(2): the receipt must state the description and location of the premises.
  • The receipt must state the amount of rent paid.
    s.5(2), Tenancy Law of Lagos State 2011 — s.5(2): the receipt must state the amount of rent paid.
  • The receipt must state the period to which the payment relates.
    s.5(2), Tenancy Law of Lagos State 2011 — s.5(2): the receipt must state the period to which the payment relates.
  • A landlord who fails to issue a receipt is liable on conviction to a fine of ₦100,000.
    s.5, Tenancy Law of Lagos State 2011 — s.5(3): a landlord who fails to issue the receipt is liable on conviction to a fine of ₦100,000.
  • Joint Revenue Board PIT Guidelines 2026, para 9.6 — minimum information for rent relief: (i) the legal tenant's name; (ii) actual rent paid and the period covered; (iii) the landlord's full name and one of mobile number, Tax ID or NIN; (iv) the property address. A receipt in the tenant's own name with all four, the signed agreement and proof of payment is what HR and tax offices ask for.
