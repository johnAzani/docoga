Rent increase notice in Nigeria — the sample letter, free PDF, the generator, and the section of the law in your state

Updated 12 November 2026 · DocOga

A rent increase in Nigeria is an offer for the next term, not an order for this one. The letter that does it properly says the current rent stands until the term ends, names the new rent in figures and words, asks for an answer in writing, and — this is the part nobody else prints — says what the law of your state actually adds: in Lagos, the tenant’s s.37 right to have the court call it unreasonable; in Enugu, the 15% cap and the two-year rule; in the FCT and most states, that the agreement governs. The same tool writes the tenant’s reply: accept, decline, or counter-offer, with the s.37 right reserved. The sample (landlord to a yearly tenant, Lagos premises)

NOTICE OF RENT REVIEW

[LANDLORD'S FULL NAME], [landlord's address]

[Date]

[TENANT'S FULL NAME], [the flat / shop / house and full address of the
premises]

Dear [Tenant's name],

RE: NOTICE OF RENT REVIEW — [2-BEDROOM FLAT, FIRST FLOOR, 12 EXAMPLE STREET,
SURULERE, LAGOS]

Your current [yearly] tenancy of the above premises runs to [31 December 2026]
at a rent of [Eight Hundred and Fifty Thousand (₦850,000) Naira Only] [per
annum].

This is written notice that, if you wish to continue in the premises after
that date, the rent from [1 January 2027] will be [Nine Hundred and Fifty
Thousand (₦950,000) Naira Only] [per annum], a change of [₦100,000 (11.8%)].

This is a proposal for the next term and not a demand for the present one: the
rent you agreed stands until [31 December 2026], and the new rent takes effect
only on your written acceptance.

Please let me have your answer in writing by [30 November 2026]. If we do not
agree a rent, the tenancy is not thereby ended; it continues on its present
terms until it is lawfully determined by the notice your agreement or the law
requires.

Nothing in this notice takes away your right under section 37 of the Lagos
State Tenancy Law 2011 to apply to the court for an order declaring an
increase unreasonable; by section 37(4) no tenant may be ejected while that
application is pending.

Yours faithfully,


______________________________
[Landlord's full name, signature and phone number]  -  Landlord / for the Landlord

Received by: ____________________ (tenant)   Signature: __________   Date:
__________

What the law actually says, state by state

StateWhat the law actually says about a rent increaseSection
LagosNo percentage cap and no fixed notice period for an increase. A sitting tenant who thinks an increase unreasonable applies to the court on Form TL11; the court weighs the general level of rents in the locality, the evidence and any special circumstances, and may order the increase changed to a stated figure (Form TL13). It is unlawful to eject the tenant while that application is pending. If the tenant refuses and nothing is agreed, the landlord’s only lawful route is the notice to quit the tenancy requires (yearly tenant: six months), the seven-day notice, then the court. Two limits nobody prints: the 2011 Law — and with it s.37 — does not apply in Apapa, Ikeja GRA, Ikoyi and Victoria Island (s.1(3)); and an increase that arrives bundled with a demand for two years upfront breaks s.4 — a sitting yearly tenant may not be asked for more than one year in advance (fine ₦100,000, s.4(5)), whatever the new figure.Tenancy Law 2011, s.37, s.37(4); s.13(1); s.1(3); s.4
EnuguThe one verified statutory cap in Nigeria. Rent “may be reviewed by the parties after every second anniversary of the tenancy … provided that the difference between the new and the existing rent shall not exceed 15% of the existing rent”. Three conditions: by agreement (a unilateral increase is ineffective), not before the second anniversary, not more than 15%. ₦400,000 let from 1 January 2025 cannot be reviewed before 1 January 2027 and may not go above ₦460,000.Landlord and Tenant (Amendment) Law 2008, s.3(b)
Abuja (FCT)No FCT equivalent of the Lagos s.37 application and no percentage cap. The tenancy agreement governs; where it is silent, the notice required is a matter of negotiation and, ultimately, of the court. The 2026 FCT Advance Rent Bill (second reading 4 Nov 2026) proposes no rent ceiling and no limit on increases.no provision; HB.2839 (a bill)
KwaraShifting a burden from the landlord to the tenant (a repair, a levy) is treated as an increase in rent whether or not the periodic sum changes.s.7
Rivers · Anambra · KanoNo percentage cap; no verified equivalent of the Lagos s.37 application; no tribunal to apply to. Rent increases are governed by the tenancy agreement. Anambra’s neighbour Enugu has a cap — that is Enugu’s law, not Anambra’s.not verified / no mechanism found

Everywhere: a rent increase is an offer for the next term, not an order for this one — “rent increment must be supported by an agreement” (Jovinco Nigeria Ltd v. Ibeozimako (2014) LPELR-23599(CA); Cobra Ltd v. Omole Estate (2000) 1 NWLR (Pt. 655) 1). Refusing it is not arrears; the tenancy continues on its present terms until it is lawfully determined. How much notice does a rent increase need? The honest answer

  • No state statute we have read fixes a notice period for a rent increase. The figures pages quote — one month, three months, six months — are the notice-to-quit periods (Lagos Tenancy Law 2011, s.13(1)), which answer a different question.
  • Why those periods still matter. If the tenant does not accept the new rent, the landlord cannot impose it; the only lawful route to a new rent with a new tenant is to determine the tenancy with the notice it requires — six months for a yearly tenant in Lagos — then the seven-day notice, then the court. A landlord who wants a higher rent from 1 January must therefore be talking by July.
  • The agreement comes first. A rent-review clause (“not less than three months’ written notice before the end of the term; by agreement”) binds both sides. The tenancy agreement generator writes one.
  • Enugu is the exception in the other direction: not a notice period but a timing rule — no review before every second anniversary — and a ceiling of 15%.

What each line of the notice does

  • The current term and rent. “Your current yearly tenancy runs to [date] at a rent of ₦…” — it fixes what is not changing, which is the first thing a tenant’s lawyer checks.
  • The proposal. The new rent in figures and words, the date it would start (the day after the term ends), the change in naira and percent, and the reason if there is one.
  • The sentence that keeps it lawful. “This is a proposal for the next term and not a demand for the present one … the new rent takes effect only on your written acceptance.” A notice that says “effective immediately” mid-term is the one the cases strike down.
  • The answer date, and what silence means. No agreement does not end the tenancy; it continues on its present terms until lawfully determined. The notice says so instead of implying an eviction.
  • The state paragraph. Lagos: s.37 and s.37(4). Enugu: s.3(b) and — if the figure is over the cap — a line saying it must come down before the notice is served. FCT and others: the agreement governs.

  • Reply in writing. Acknowledge, say whether you accept, counter-offer if you wish, and ask for the landlord’s answer in writing. A WhatsApp message is writing; keep it.
  • In Lagos, the s.37 application (Form TL11) asks the court to declare the increase unreasonable; no ejection is lawful while it is pending (s.37(4)). Bring your receipts and evidence of rents nearby — two or three comparable rents on your street, dated, is what the court measures the increase against. Not in Apapa, Ikeja GRA, Ikoyi or Victoria Island, where the 2011 Law does not apply (s.1(3)).

Questions people ask

Can a landlord increase rent in the middle of a tenancy in Nigeria?

Not unilaterally. A rent increase takes effect only by agreement — the cases call a unilateral increase ineffective — so an increase properly belongs to the next term, proposed in writing before the current one ends. A rent-review clause in the agreement can allow a review on notice during a longer term.

How much notice must a landlord give before increasing rent?

No Nigerian state statute we have read fixes one. The agreement governs; the notice-to-quit periods (one month, three months, six months in Lagos by tenancy type) are what the landlord needs if the tenant refuses and the tenancy is to be ended instead. Giving the proposal several months before renewal is both practical and the only way a yearly tenancy can lawfully be re-let at a new rent on time.

Is there a percentage limit on rent increases in Nigeria?

Only one that we have verified: Enugu State, where the Landlord and Tenant (Amendment) Law 2008 (s.3(b)) allows a review only after every second anniversary and caps the difference at 15% of the existing rent. Lagos has no cap but gives the tenant the s.37 application; the FCT, Rivers, Anambra and Kano have neither.

Is it true a landlord cannot increase rent by more than 20%?

No. The 20% figure is a House of Representatives motion of 23 October 2025 urging that increases be kept within 20% of the existing rent — a resolution, not a law; it binds nobody. The FCT bill at second reading (HB.2839, 4 November 2026) caps advance rent at twelve months and proposes no limit on increases. Quote a section to your landlord, not a headline: in Lagos that is s.37; in Enugu, s.3(b).

What should a Lagos tenant gather before challenging an increase under s.37?

The court’s first measure is the general level of rents for comparable premises in the locality, so note two or three current rents for similar flats on nearby streets (listings, neighbours, an agent’s quote) with the date, keep every rent receipt, and keep the landlord’s notice and your written reply. What happens if the tenant refuses the new rent?

The tenancy continues on its present terms. The landlord may negotiate, or determine the tenancy with the notice it requires and the court process; the landlord may not treat the refusal as arrears or lock the tenant out.

How should a tenant reply to a rent increase letter?

In writing, before the date the landlord asked for: refer to the notice by its date, say whether you accept, decline or counter-offer (with your figure), state that the tenancy continues on its present terms until a rent is agreed and that the letter is not a notice to quit, and — in Lagos — reserve the s.37 right to ask the court to declare the increase unreasonable. Keep paying the current rent and keep the receipts.

Does the notice need a lawyer?

Not for a residential renewal. A solicitor is the right step when the sum is large, the tenant disputes it, or a Lagos s.37 application is coming. DocOga is not a law firm and this page is general information, not legal advice.
