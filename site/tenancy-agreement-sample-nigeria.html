The sample (residential, yearly tenant, Lagos wording)

RESIDENTIAL TENANCY AGREEMENT

Made this [1st] day of [January 2027]

THIS TENANCY AGREEMENT is made between

[LANDLORD'S FULL NAME] of [landlord's address] (Tel: [phone]) (hereinafter
called "the Landlord", which expression shall where the context so admits
include the Landlord's heirs, successors-in-title and assigns)

AND

[TENANT'S FULL NAME] of [tenant's address] (Tel: [phone]) (hereinafter called
"the Tenant").

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner or is lawfully entitled to grant a tenancy
of all that property known, described and situate at [2-bedroom flat, first
floor], [12 Example Street, Surulere], [Lagos] State (hereinafter called "the
Demised Premises").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES as follows:

1. TERM. The Landlord agrees to let and the Tenant agrees to take the Demised
Premises for a term of [12] months certain, commencing on [1 January 2027] and
ending on [31 December 2027], renewable by mutual agreement of the parties.

2. RENT. The Tenant shall pay to the Landlord a rent of [₦850,000 (Eight
Hundred and Fifty Thousand Naira)] per annum, payable in advance, without
deduction except as may be permitted by law.

3. CAUTION FEE / DEPOSIT. The Tenant has paid a caution fee (security deposit)
of [₦100,000 (One Hundred Thousand Naira)], refundable at the expiration or
earlier termination of the tenancy, subject to deductions for any damage to
the Demised Premises beyond normal wear and tear.

4. USE OF PREMISES. The Tenant shall use the Demised Premises for residential
purposes only and shall not use the Premises or permit it to be used for any
illegal, immoral or hazardous purpose.

5. NO ASSIGNMENT OR SUBLETTING. The Tenant shall not assign, sublet, or part
with possession of the Demised Premises or any part thereof without the prior
written consent of the Landlord.

6. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE. The Landlord shall keep the roof and external
walls of the Demised Premises in good structural repair. The Tenant shall keep
the interior, sanitary fittings and installations in good and tenantable
condition, and shall be liable for any damage caused by the negligence of the
Tenant, his guests or invitees.

7. UTILITIES. The Tenant shall pay all charges for electricity, water, waste
disposal and other utilities consumed on the Demised Premises during the
tenancy.

8. QUIET ENJOYMENT. The Landlord covenants that the Tenant, upon paying the
rent and observing the covenants herein, shall peacefully hold and enjoy the
Demised Premises during the tenancy without any interruption by the Landlord
or any person lawfully claiming under him.

9. ALTERATIONS. The Tenant shall not make any structural alteration or
addition to the Demised Premises without the prior written consent of the
Landlord.

10. INSPECTION. The Landlord or his authorised agent may enter the Demised
Premises to inspect its condition upon giving the Tenant reasonable prior
notice.

11. NOTICE TO QUIT. Either party may terminate this tenancy by serving on the
other party written notice to quit of [six months], in accordance with the
Tenancy Law applicable in [Lagos] State.

12. RENEWAL AND RENT REVIEW. The parties may renew this tenancy upon terms to
be agreed, and the rent may be reviewed upon renewal by mutual agreement.

13. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION. This agreement shall be governed by the
laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria and the tenancy law applicable in
[Lagos] State, and the parties submit to the jurisdiction of the courts of
that state.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have executed this agreement on the date first
above written.


______________________________
LANDLORD — Signature / Date

______________________________
TENANT — Signature / Date

______________________________
WITNESS 1 — Name / Signature / Date

______________________________
WITNESS 2 — Name / Signature / Date

What each clause does — and why it is there

  • Term — the length of the tenancy and its start and end dates — a fixed term ends by itself; what follows is renewal, not holding over by default.
  • Rent — the figure in numbers and words, the period it buys, and that it is paid in advance. In Lagos, s.4 of the Tenancy Law 2011 caps the advance at one year (yearly tenant) or six months (monthly tenant).
  • Caution fee / deposit — names the deposit as refundable and says what may be deducted — the clause that makes a refund arguable at the end.
  • Use of premises — residential only; no illegal, immoral or hazardous use.
  • No assignment or subletting — no handing the flat to someone else without the landlord's written consent.
  • Repairs and maintenance — the usual split: landlord — roof and external walls; tenant — interior, fittings and damage by his own people.
  • Utilities — who pays electricity, water and waste — the tenant, for what is consumed during the tenancy.
  • Quiet enjoyment — the landlord's promise not to interfere while the tenant pays and keeps the covenants — the clause self-help breaches.
  • Alterations — no structural changes without written consent.
  • Inspection — the landlord may inspect on reasonable prior notice — not walk in.
  • Notice to quit — the notice either side must give to end the tenancy. Where the agreement is silent the statutory minimums apply (table below); what you write here governs instead.
  • Renewal and rent review — renewal and any new rent are by agreement — there is no automatic increase.
  • Governing law and jurisdiction — the laws of Nigeria and the tenancy law of the state where the premises sit; disputes go to that state's courts.

Before you sign: the three rules that decide whether it holds up

  • The advance-rent cap. Section 4 of the Lagos Tenancy Law 2011 makes it unlawful for a landlord or agent to demand or receive more than one year's rent in advance from a yearly tenant, or six months from a monthly tenant — and for the tenant to pay it.
  • Notice is what you write. The statutory minimums apply only where the agreement is silent. If you want three months' notice on a yearly tenancy, clause 11 must say three months.
  • State law, not one national law. Lagos runs the Tenancy Law 2011; the FCT the Recovery of Premises Act; Rivers, Edo, Kwara, Cross River, Kano and the rest their own recovery or rent-control laws. Clause 13 names the state; the state guides below give the sections. Where the Lagos law applies: the Lagos Tenancy Law 2011 covers most of Lagos State, but s.1(3) excludes four areas: Apapa, Ikeja GRA, Ikoyi and Victoria Island (plus residential premises of educational institutions, emergency shelters, care/hospice, hospital and mental-health facilities, and rehabilitative or therapeutic treatment settings).

These are the minimums where the agreement is silent. Where a written agreement stipulates a notice period, that stipulation generally governs — so the notice you actually want must be written into the agreement.
