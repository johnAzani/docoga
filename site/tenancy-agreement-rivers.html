Expiry of rent does not end the tenancy. A tenancy is determined by a valid notice (or effluxion of a fixed term) — not by the rent running out. A landlord who treats the end of the paid period as the end of the tenancy and moves to eject is acting unlawfully.

What the law actually says in Rivers

The statute: Recovery of Premises Law — Cap. 109, Laws of Rivers State of Nigeria, 1999. Commenced 1999.

Governsquit notices and the procedure for recovering possession.

Where a dispute goes

Recovery proceedings run through the courts of Rivers State — a notice to quit in practice issues in the name of the Magistrate Court of Rivers State, in the magisterial district where the premises sit (for example, the Port Harcourt Magisterial District). We have not read the primary text establishing a Rivers Rent Tribunal, and we do not assert that one exists.

Advance rent

We have found no Rivers equivalent of the Lagos s.4 advance-rent cap. On the statutory starting point above, rent in Rivers is not even due until it is in arrear. Whatever advance is agreed should be written into the tenancy agreement before money moves — and note that a written advance term displaces the s.75(1) default.

Other protections worth knowing

  • A landlord may not resort to self-help — no lock-outs, no removal of goods, no disconnection. Unlawful eviction sounds in damages.
  • A defect in the issue, content or service of a notice vitiates the whole proceeding.
  • The date of service is the material date, not the date written on the notice.

What your Rivers State (Port Harcourt) agreement should lock down

  • The notice period you actually want. Courts uphold what the agreement says — the statutory defaults apply only “where there is no stipulation”. If you want three months, write three months.
  • The rent and the advance — exact amount in figures and words, and how much is payable in advance.
  • The caution fee — amount, purpose, and the refund terms. This is the most disputed money in Nigerian tenancies.
  • The parties and the property — full names, addresses, and a clear description of the premises.
  • Covenants — repairs, utilities, subletting, use of the premises, and renewal terms.

Questions people ask

Is a written tenancy agreement compulsory in Rivers State (Port Harcourt)?

A tenancy can exist without one — but without it you have no agreed rent record, no caution-fee terms, and the statutory notice defaults govern. A two-minute written agreement is cheaper than any dispute.

Does the agreement override the notice periods in the law?

Generally yes — the statutory periods apply where the agreement is silent. Write the notice you want into the agreement.
