Demand letter for unpaid rent in Nigeria — the sample letter, free PDF, and the arrears rule in your state

The sample (landlord to tenant, rent in arrears, Lagos premises)

DEMAND LETTER — UNPAID RENT

[LANDLORD'S FULL NAME], [landlord's address]

[Date]

[TENANT'S FULL NAME], [the flat / shop / house and full address of the
premises]

Dear Sir/Madam,

RE: DEMAND FOR PAYMENT OF [FOUR HUNDRED AND TWENTY-FIVE THOUSAND (₦425,000)
NAIRA ONLY]

We refer to [the rent of ₦425,000 for the period 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027
in respect of the two-bedroom flat at 12 Example Street, Surulere, Lagos],
which fell due on [1 July 2026]. Despite the passage of time, the said sum
remains wholly unpaid.

DEMAND is hereby made for the immediate payment of the sum of [Four Hundred
and Twenty-Five Thousand (₦425,000) Naira Only], being the amount outstanding
and owed to us.

TAKE NOTICE that if the said sum is not paid within [14] days of the date of
this letter, we shall, without further reference to you, instruct our
solicitors to commence legal proceedings for the recovery of the debt, plus
interest and costs, to the full extent permitted by law.

We look forward to your prompt settlement.

Yours faithfully,


______________________________
[Landlord's full name, signature and phone number]  -  or: [Agent / solicitor's name], for the landlord

A demand letter is the written record that the rent was asked for before
anything else was done. It is not a notice to quit: ending the tenancy still
takes the notice your agreement or your state's law requires, then the seven
days' notice of the owner's intention to recover possession, then the court.
Keep a copy and proof of delivery.

What each line of the letter is for

  • The sum, in figures and in words. A demand that names a different figure in the heading and the body is the first thing a tenant’s lawyer attacks. The generator writes both from one number.
  • What the money is for and when it fell due. “The rent of ₦… for the period … in respect of …, which fell due on …” — the period and the due date are what turn a complaint into arrears that a court can count.
  • The demand itself. One sentence, in capitals, asking for immediate payment of the amount outstanding — the sentence the court reads as the demand.
  • The days to pay. Fourteen days is the usual figure; seven is common where the agreement says rent is due on a fixed day. The letter must give a period — a demand with no deadline is a reminder.
  • What follows. Proceedings for the debt, interest and costs — the recovery of the money. Recovering the premises is a separate ladder: the notice to quit, the seven-day notice, the court order. The letter says so and promises nothing it cannot do.
  • Who signs. The landlord, or an agent or solicitor for the landlord. Keep a copy and proof of delivery — a WhatsApp photo of the signed letter with the tenant’s reply is proof.

Rent in arrears, state by state — when the law lets a tenancy lapse without a quit notice

StateWhat the law says about rent in arrearsSection
LagosA monthly tenant six months in arrears: the tenancy lapses and the court makes an order for possession and arrears on proof — no quit notice needed. A quarterly or half-yearly tenant one year in arrears: the same.Tenancy Law 2011, s.13(2), s.13(3)
FCT AbujaNo arrears shortcut. The Recovery of Premises Act has no equivalent of the Lagos rule — serve the notice to quit, then the seven-day Form E notice, then the court.Recovery of Premises Act, s.7
Cross RiverA monthly tenant three months in arrears: the tenancy determines and the court shall, on the landlord’s application, make an order for possession and arrears.Rent Control and Recovery of Premises Law, s.15 proviso; Schedule 3(a)
KwaraA monthly tenant three months in arrears: the same. And under Chapter L4, rent may be demanded or recovered only once it is in arrear.Rent Control and Recovery of Residential Premises Law 1995, s.17 proviso; Landlord and Tenant Law, Ch. L4
EdoRent lawfully due and in arrear for one month after it became due is a ground on which the tribunal may order possession — the shortest trigger on the shelf.Rent Control and Recovery of Residential Premises Edict 1977
RiversRent is payable in arrears — at the end of the period — unless the parties agree otherwise. Advance rent is whatever the agreement says.Landlord and Tenant Law, Cap. 75, s.75(1)
Anambra · Enugu · KanoNo arrears shortcut verified. After the quit notice expires, a further written notice of the owner’s intention to apply to court of not less than seven clear days — and it cannot be issued before the quit notice expires.Landlord and Tenant Law (Anambra, Enugu); Kano recovery law

Where a state is not in the table, the ordinary rule applies: the demand letter, then the notice to quit your agreement or the state law requires, then the seven-day notice, then the court. Every section here is on the state tenancy-law pages, with the date it was checked.

The ladder after the letter (and the step that is an offence)

  • 1. The demand letter — this page. It recovers the money, or it becomes the first exhibit.
  • 2. The notice to quit — the period your agreement names or, where it is silent, the state minimum (one week to six months, by tenancy type). In Lagos, Cross River and Kwara the arrears rules in the table skip this step once the arrears reach the statutory period; in the FCT they never do.
  • 3. The seven-day notice of the owner’s intention to apply to court to recover possession (Form E in the FCT, Edo, Cross River and Kwara; Form TL5 in Lagos) — and it cannot be served before the quit notice expires.
  • 4. The court order. A landlord may not resort to self-help: no changed locks, no removed roof, no thugs. In the FCT, once the seven days expire the occupier is a statutory tenant removable only by a lawful court order; in Lagos, forcible ejection is an offence under s.44 of the Tenancy Law 2011 — fine up to ₦250,000 or up to six months’ imprisonment.

If you are the tenant who received one

  • Check the figure against your receipts. In a possession claim built on arrears, the tenant’s receipts are the answer — in Lagos a landlord who did not issue one has committed an offence (s.5(3), fine ₦100,000). If you paid and have no receipt, your bank transfer narration and your messages are the next best evidence.
  • A demand letter is not an eviction. It does not end the tenancy and it does not shorten the notice you are owed. Reply in writing — pay, propose dates, or dispute the figure — and keep the reply.
  • In Rivers and Kwara, check when rent is even due. Rent is payable in arrears unless your agreement says otherwise; a demand for rent that is not yet due is not a demand for arrears.
  • Watch the statutory periods. Six months’ arrears for a monthly tenant in Lagos, three in Cross River and Kwara, one in Edo: at those points the law stops requiring a quit notice. Pay something, in writing, before you reach them.

Questions people ask

How do I write a demand letter for unpaid rent in Nigeria?

Name the premises, the period the rent covers and the date it fell due; state the sum in figures and words; demand immediate payment; give a number of days (fourteen is usual); say that proceedings for the debt, interest and costs follow if it is not paid; sign and date it, and keep proof of delivery. Is a demand letter the same as a notice to quit?

No. A demand letter asks for money and records that it was asked for. A notice to quit ends the tenancy after the period your agreement or the state law requires. One can follow the other; neither replaces the other — except where a state’s arrears rule (Lagos s.13(2)–(3), the Cross River and Kwara provisos) lets the tenancy lapse on the arrears themselves.

How many days should I give the tenant to pay?

The letter must give a period; fourteen days is the common figure and seven is used where the agreement fixes a rent day. The period in the letter does not shorten any notice period the tenancy itself requires.

Can I lock the tenant out if the demand is ignored?

No. Self-help is unlawful in every state on this page — in Lagos it is an offence under s.44 of the Tenancy Law 2011. The route is the notice to quit (where still required), the seven-day notice, and a court order for possession and arrears.

Does a demand letter need a lawyer?

Not for the first demand on a residential tenancy. A solicitor’s letter adds weight and is the right step when the sum is large, the tenant disputes it, or the court is the next stop. DocOga is not a law firm and this page is general information, not legal advice.
