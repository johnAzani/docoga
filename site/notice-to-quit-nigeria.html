Quit notice in Nigeria — sample letter, how to write it, and how many months

A notice to quit (quit notice) is the formal letter that starts a lawful end to a tenancy. Get the period wrong, or serve it on the wrong person, and the court will throw the case out — months wasted. Below: a sample you can copy, how many months each tenancy type requires in Nigeria, how to serve it, and a generator that produces a correctly-formatted notice in about two minutes.

Sample quit notice letter (copy the wording)

For a yearly tenant in Lagos where the agreement is silent (six months). Change the tenancy type and the period for a monthly (1 month) or quarterly (3 months) tenant — the table below.

[Date of service]

To: [Tenant's full name]
[Flat / shop / house and full address of the premises]

Notice to quit

I, [Landlord's full name], the landlord of the premises you occupy as a yearly tenant at [address of the premises], hereby give you six months' notice to quit and deliver up possession of the said premises to me on or before [expiry date].

Take notice that if you remain in possession after that date, I shall serve on you the seven days' notice of owner's intention to recover possession and apply to the court for an order of possession, together with any arrears of rent and mesne profits.

Dated this ____ day of ______________ 20____.

______________________________
[Landlord's name, signature and phone number]
or: [Agent / solicitor's name], for the landlord, under written authority dated [date before this notice]

How many months? The minimums

Type of tenancyMinimum notice to quit
Tenant at will / weekly1 week
Monthly1 month
Quarterly3 months
Half-yearly3 months
Yearly6 months

These are the minimums where the agreement is silent. Where a written agreement stipulates a notice period, that stipulation generally governs — so the notice you actually want must be written into the agreement.

How to write a quit notice — the six things it must carry

  • Who is giving it — the landlord by name, or an agent/solicitor who holds the landlord's written authority dated before the notice.
  • Who it is to and which premises — the tenant's name and the exact flat, shop or house (the address as it appears on the rent receipt or agreement).
  • The type of tenancy — weekly, monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or yearly, because that fixes the length of notice (table above).
  • The notice period and the expiry date — "six months' notice … to deliver up possession on or before [date]". Where the written agreement fixes its own period, use that one.
  • Date and signature — dated the day it is served and signed by the person giving it.
  • Plain language. It is a notice, not a lawsuit: no insults, no threats beyond the lawful next step (the 7 days' notice and the court). Reasons are optional; the period and the date are not.

Serving it in Lagos: hand it to the tenant; or leave it with an adult living in the premises; or send it by courier; or, where the tenant cannot be found, paste it on a conspicuous part of the premises and photograph it (Lagos Tenancy Law 2011, ss.13–16). Keep a dated copy — proof of service wins or loses the case later.

The two notices every lawful eviction needs

  • 1. The notice to quit — see the table. It must state the premises, the date the tenant is to give up possession, and be signed by the landlord or by an agent/solicitor with written authority dated before the notice is issued.
  • 2. The 7 days' notice of the owner's intention to recover possession — served after the notice to quit has expired and the tenant is still there. Only then can the landlord apply to court.
  • 3. Court, not self-help. Possession is recovered through the court. In Lagos that is the Magistrates' Court or the High Court, depending on the premises.

When no notice to quit is needed at all

If the fixed term has already expired and the tenant is holding over, no notice to quit is required — the landlord serves only the 7 days' notice of intention to recover possession and proceeds to court. This surprises most landlords: the long notice applies to ending a tenancy that is still running, not to one that has already finished.

Arrears: when a tenant loses the right to a quit notice

Under s.13(2)–(3) of the Lagos Tenancy Law 2011, a monthly tenant who is six months in arrears loses the entitlement to a quit notice, and a quarterly or half-yearly tenant who is one year in arrears loses it too. The landlord can go to court on proof of the arrears. Never let arrears run — and landlords, follow the lawful process: self-help is a crime (below).

Self-help is a criminal offence in Lagos

Section 44 makes it an offence to demolish, alter or modify a building in order to eject a tenant without the court's approval, to attempt to forcibly eject a tenant, to threaten or molest a tenant with a view to ejecting them, or wilfully to damage the premises. The penalty is a fine of up to ₦250,000 or up to six months' imprisonment. Roof-removal, lock-changing and removing a tenant's doors all fall inside this section.

Questions people ask

How do I write a quit notice in Nigeria?

State who is giving it (the landlord, or an agent with written authority), the tenant's name and the exact premises, the type of tenancy, the length of notice and the date the tenant must give up possession, then date and sign it. Serve it personally, through an adult living there, by courier, or by pasting it on the premises — and keep a dated copy. The sample and the free generator on this page produce exactly that.

Can my landlord give me less than six months' notice?

If your written agreement specifies a shorter (or longer) notice, that clause generally governs — the statutory minimums apply only where the agreement is silent. Read your agreement before you react to a notice.

How do I count the notice period?

Traditionally the notice had to expire at the end of a complete period of the tenancy; the modern Lagos rule allows expiry on or after the current term ends. When you are the one giving notice, give more than the minimum — a generous notice is rarely challenged, a short one is.

Does a notice to quit mean I must leave immediately?

No. It starts the process. If you stay past the expiry date the landlord must still serve the 7 days' notice and then obtain a court order. But you do owe rent (or mesne profits) for the period you remain.

What if the notice was signed by an agent?

That is fine if the agent had written authority from the landlord dated before the notice was issued. An agent signing without authority is a common reason notices fail.

Can I be evicted without going to court?

No. In Lagos, forcible ejection is an offence under s.44 — fine up to ₦250,000 or up to six months' imprisonment. Only the court can order possession.
