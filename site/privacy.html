Privacy Policy

DocOga · Last updated: 18 September 2026 · Abuja, Nigeria

The short version: your documents are created on your own device. We do not see, receive, or store them. No account, no upload.

1. Information we do NOT collect

DocOga is a browser-based tool. Everything you type — names, addresses, rent amounts, property details — is processed and saved only on your device (browser local storage). It is never transmitted to us or to any server. If you clear your browser data, your saved documents are deleted from your device.

2. Website analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand aggregate usage — pages visited, approximate location, device type. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally. You can block analytics with any standard browser setting or extension.

3. Payments

Payments for the Oga Pass are processed by our licensed payment provider (Flutterwave). Your card details, bank details and payment information are handled entirely by the provider on their secure platform — DocOga never sees or stores them. The provider's own privacy policy applies to payment data.

4. Communication with us

If you contact us on WhatsApp or by email, we keep the message history so we can help you. We do not send unsolicited messages and we do not sell or share your contact with anyone.

5. Children

DocOga is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

6. Your rights

Because we hold essentially no personal data, most requests are simple. If you have any privacy question or request, contact us and we will respond promptly.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

8. Contact

WhatsApp: tap to chat (see the WhatsApp button on our homepage) · DocOga, Abuja, Nigeria.
