Privacy Policy

DocOga · Last updated: 13 October 2026 · Abuja, Nigeria

The short version: your documents are created on your own device. We do not see, receive, or store them. Free users need no account at all. The one exception is a small team account, held only when your firm pays for the Business licence (see section 5).

1. Information we do NOT collect

DocOga is a browser-based tool. Everything you type — names, addresses, rent amounts, property details — is processed and saved only on your device (browser local storage). It is never transmitted to us or to any server. If you clear your browser data, your saved documents are deleted from your device. This is equally true for documents produced while signed in to a Business team.

2. Website analytics

Cloudflare Web Analytics. The site is served through Cloudflare, which may count page views with its privacy-first Web Analytics beacon. It sets no cookies, uses no fingerprinting and stores no personal identifiers; it reports aggregate counts only — how many visits a page received, the referring website, country and browser type. Nothing you type into a document tool is ever included.

Google Analytics is currently disabled in this site build. No Google Analytics tag is loaded and no page-view or interaction data is sent to Google. Pages using the first-party privacy manager expire accessible `_ga` cookies left by the older implementation; while Analytics is disabled, the manager does not request or write an Analytics preference. The persistent Privacy choices control reports that Analytics is off.

If DocOga enables the dedicated Google Analytics 4 property in a later release, it will be optional: the tag will not load until you actively choose Accept analytics. Choosing Reject analytics on the first prompt leaves all document tools available and keeps the Analytics library unloaded. The persistent Privacy choices control will then let you review or change your choice. A saved choice expires after 180 days. If you later withdraw acceptance, the site records the rejection in browser storage where available; if the Google tag has already loaded, it updates Google consent to denied. The site stops sending subsequent page views and removes first-party Analytics cookies on a best-effort basis. A request already sent cannot be recalled, and withdrawal does not erase data Google already received.

If enabled, the intended measurement is limited to page names and allowlisted DocOga app routes, basic browser/device information, approximate location, and GA4's built-in session/engagement timing signals. Other interaction metadata may include scrolls, outbound-link clicks, and form identifiers (not field values) only if those property settings are separately enabled after review. The site is designed not to send document contents, names entered into forms, Oga Pass codes, payment references, raw URL fragments, or non-allowlisted query parameters to Analytics. Only fixed, approved `utm_source` and `utm_medium` labels may be retained; campaign names, content, IDs and search terms are excluded. External referrers are reduced to their origin; the payment-success page remains untagged. Analytics and advertising-personalisation signals are configured separately, and the intended settings disable Google Signals and ad-personalisation signals.

For visitors outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google says it uses raw IP addresses briefly for basic service functions, spam detection and coarse location, then discards them after use without associating them with Google Analytics user identifiers. This describes Google's stated handling, not a promise that data stays in Nigeria; linked Google products or different regional settings may affect processing and must be checked before activation.

Google may process Analytics data outside Nigeria. Analytics will remain disabled until DocOga has verified and documented the applicable Nigeria Data Protection Act transfer basis and safeguards, Google’s relevant contract terms, property data-sharing and linked-product settings, retention, and measurement controls. This notice will be updated with the confirmed data flow before Analytics is enabled. See the Google Privacy Policy for Google's own handling of information.

3. Payments

Payments for the Oga Pass and Business licences are processed by our licensed payment provider (Flutterwave). Your card details, bank details and payment information are handled entirely by the provider on their secure platform — DocOga never sees or stores them. The provider's own privacy policy applies to payment data. We store only the reference, amount and status needed to run the service (for example, to activate your licence or show you your invoices).

4. Communication with us

If you contact us on WhatsApp or by email (including business@docoga.com.ng), we keep the message history so we can help you. We do not send unsolicited messages and we do not sell or share your contact with anyone.

5. Business teams

When a firm pays for the DocOga Business licence, a small team account is created. For that account we store: the firm or company name and optional branding (name, tagline, logo, address and footer line), the email addresses of invited seats, the licence and invoice status (amount, method, date, payment reference), a count of documents generated per month against the team's allowance (an aggregate number — not the documents themselves), and a hashed sign-in token. We do not store the content of any document any team member creates — documents are produced entirely on each member’s own device and never uploaded, exactly as in section 1. Sign-in links are emailed, work once, and expire after 10 minutes; no passwords are stored. Seat emails can be removed by the team owner at any time from the team dashboard, and we delete a team account on request to business@docoga.com.ng — apart from payment records we are required to retain for tax purposes, which are kept separately.

6. Children

DocOga is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

7. Your rights

Because we hold essentially no personal data for free users, most requests are simple. Business team owners can manage their own team account in the dashboard; for anything else, contact business@docoga.com.ng and we will respond promptly.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
