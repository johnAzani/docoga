Privacy Policy

DocOga · Last updated: 11 October 2026 · Abuja, Nigeria

The short version: your documents are created on your own device. We do not see, receive, or store them. Free users need no account at all. The one exception is a small team account, held only when your firm pays for the Business licence (see section 5).

1. Information we do NOT collect

DocOga is a browser-based tool. Everything you type — names, addresses, rent amounts, property details — is processed and saved only on your device (browser local storage). It is never transmitted to us or to any server. If you clear your browser data, your saved documents are deleted from your device. This is equally true for documents produced while signed in to a Business team.

2. Website analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand aggregate usage — pages visited, approximate location, device type. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally. You can block analytics with any standard browser setting or extension.

3. Payments

Payments for the Oga Pass and Business licences are processed by our licensed payment provider (Flutterwave). Your card details, bank details and payment information are handled entirely by the provider on their secure platform — DocOga never sees or stores them. The provider's own privacy policy applies to payment data. We store only the reference, amount and status needed to run the service (for example, to activate your licence or show you your invoices).

4. Communication with us

If you contact us on WhatsApp or by email (including business@docoga.com.ng), we keep the message history so we can help you. We do not send unsolicited messages and we do not sell or share your contact with anyone.

5. Business teams

When a firm pays for the DocOga Business licence, a small team account is created. For that account we store: the firm or company name and optional branding (name, tagline, logo, address and footer line), the email addresses of invited seats, the licence and invoice status (amount, method, date, payment reference), and a hashed sign-in token. We do not store the content of any document any team member creates — documents are produced entirely on each member’s own device and never uploaded, exactly as in section 1. Sign-in links are emailed, work once, and expire after 10 minutes; no passwords are stored. Seat emails can be removed by the team owner at any time from the team dashboard, and we delete a team account on request to business@docoga.com.ng — apart from payment records we are required to retain for tax purposes, which are kept separately.

6. Children

DocOga is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

7. Your rights

Because we hold essentially no personal data for free users, most requests are simple. Business team owners can manage their own team account in the dashboard; for anything else, contact business@docoga.com.ng and we will respond promptly.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

9. Contact

WhatsApp (Oga Pass individuals): see the WhatsApp button on our homepage · Business teams: business@docoga.com.ng · DocOga, Abuja, Nigeria.
