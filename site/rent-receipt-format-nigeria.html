Rent receipt format in Nigeria — the five things the law requires

Nigerian rent is paid in advance, often a whole year in one transfer. That makes the receipt the single most important document a tenant holds — it is the proof of payment in any dispute, and the landlord's clean record too. In Lagos it is not optional: a landlord who refuses a receipt can be fined. Here is the exact format, and a generator that produces one in about two minutes.

What the law requires on a rent receipt (Lagos)

The Lagos Tenancy Law 2011 obliges every landlord, on payment of rent, to issue a rent payment receipt to the tenant. The receipt must state:

  • the date the rent was received;
  • the names and addresses of the landlord and the tenant;
  • the description and location of the premises the payment relates to;
  • the amount of rent paid — and put it in words as well as figures, so it cannot be altered;
  • the period the payment covers (for example, one year from 1 October 2026).

A landlord who fails to issue a rent payment receipt is liable on conviction to a fine of ₦100,000 (s.5(3)).

A receipt that holds up looks like this

RENT RECEIPT — No. 0142

Date received:   1 October 2026
Received from:   Chinedu Okafor, 12 Bode Thomas St, Surulere, Lagos
Of:              Landlord — Mrs A. Bello, 4 Adeola St, Ikeja, Lagos
The sum of:      One Million Five Hundred Thousand Naira only (₦1,500,000)
Being:           Rent for the self-contained apartment at Flat 2, 12 Bode
                 Thomas Street, Surulere, Lagos
Period covered:  1 October 2026 to 30 September 2027 (one year)
Paid by:         Bank transfer — ref 90827364

Signed:  ______________________   (landlord or authorised agent)
         Mrs A. Bello

Other money needs its own receipt

  • Caution fee / security deposit — a separate receipt, never folded into the rent figure. This is what makes the deposit refundable in practice.
  • Service charges and facility charges — also receipted separately.
  • A written account every six months. Where a landlord collects a deposit, service or facility charges, the tenant is entitled to a written account at least every six months of how that money was spent.
  • Agent collections — whoever collects must receipt. Your agreement should name who is authorised to collect rent.

Cash, transfer, and proof

  • Bank transfer is the safest record — but the transfer alone is not a receipt. Ask for the receipt anyway; the transfer reference belongs on it.
  • After any transfer, send one WhatsApp message stating the amount, what it covers and the date. A read receipt creates a dated record.
  • Never pay cash without a signed receipt in your hand first. Hand over the money and the receipt in the same moment.

Questions people ask

Is my landlord obliged to give me a receipt?

In Lagos, yes — the Tenancy Law 2011 requires a rent payment receipt on payment of rent, and a landlord who refuses is liable on conviction to a ₦100,000 fine (s.5(3)). Even where a state's law is silent, a landlord who will not receipt a payment is a red flag no tenant should accept.

Can a rent receipt be handwritten?

Yes — what matters is the content and the signature, not the printing. A clean printed receipt simply removes the arguments about what was written.

I paid an agent, not the landlord. Who issues the receipt?

Whoever collects the money must receipt it. Your tenancy agreement should identify who is authorised to collect rent, and the agent should be collecting on the landlord's behalf.

Does a receipt need a stamp or seal?

No stamp or seal is required for the receipt to be valid evidence. Some agreements and instruments attract stamp duty separately — that is a different tax, handled at state or federal level, and it does not make an unstamped receipt worthless.

What if my landlord refuses to issue one?

Ask in writing (WhatsApp counts), quoting that the law requires it. If they still refuse, keep your transfer evidence and the messages — and in Lagos this is itself a breach you can raise with the Rent Tribunal or a lawyer.
